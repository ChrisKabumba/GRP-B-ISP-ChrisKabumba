About Asthma Classifier

Empowering healthcare with intelligent asthma prediction technology.

Our Mission

Our goal is to make early detection and management of asthma symptoms accessible and reliable. Using machine learning technology, our platform analyzes health data to help users and professionals predict asthma severity and take proactive steps toward healthier living.

Our Vision

We envision a world where artificial intelligence assists medical professionals in delivering faster, more accurate respiratory health insights — improving patient outcomes and supporting preventive care.
